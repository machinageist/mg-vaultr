## Verdict: PASS

**Summary:** The strongest quality is truthfulness under degradation: §3.3 segment 9 forces a literal index-freshness word from an exhaustive renderer with no default arm, §3.2/§3.6/§6.4 withhold the recover action entirely when a journal base predates current source, and §3.7 makes graph and Canvas textual-by-default with `--text` always available — every auto-fail rule is addressed by name and none triggers. The most critical gap is in the anchor lens: §3.2 (Tabs), §3.4 (counts) and §5.3 bind `gt`/`{count}gt`/`gT` and `]c`/`[c`, none of which any of the five routing namespaces in §3.4 can claim, so they contradict both rule 5 ("everything else goes to D's `dispatch` verbatim") and the §5.1 test `router_never_shadows_buffer_keys`. Secondary: §6.1 says "the message log is in-memory only" while §3.2.3 and §4.4 persist message history to XDG state, and §4.5/§6.2 assert `nucleo-matcher` is MIT while §8 Q8 says its licensing needs checking (it is MPL-2.0).
